Common Questions

Your SMP Questions Answered

Does it hurt?

Most clients describe it as no worse than a tattoo on a less sensitive area — and Simon allows breaks throughout. Many clients chat or listen to music during sessions. EMLA numbing cream is available if needed.

How long does it last?

3 to 8 years before any noticeable fading. And here's our promise: every HairTattoo.ca client gets 12 months of free touchups, plus 60% off touchups every year after that.

How many sessions are needed?

Most clients need 2 to 3 sessions, spaced about 1–2 weeks apart. We build the result in layers — a foundation layer first, then darker tones to create realistic 3D depth.

Is it permanent?

The pigment is long-lasting but designed to soften gradually over years — that way your SMP ages naturally with you, never looking too dark or out of place as you get older.

How much does it cost?

Cost depends on your hair-loss pattern and how much coverage you need. It's far more affordable than a hair transplant. Your free consultation includes an exact quote — call us anytime at (647) 692-9114 for details.

Can I go back to work right after?

Yes. There's slight redness for 24–48 hours after each session, but no downtime. Avoid sweating heavily, sun, or steam rooms for the first few days — otherwise, life as normal.

What if I'm thinning, not bald?

SMP density-shading is perfect for diffuse thinning — it adds visible scalp coverage without obvious dots. Works well for both men and women with thinning hair.

Will it look real up close?

Yes. Our 3D layering technique places micro-dots at varying tones and depths so it reads as real follicles even at close conversation distance. See our before & afters above.
